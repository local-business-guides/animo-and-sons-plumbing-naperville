Questions and details to gather

Use this short checklist before you call or arrange a visit:

  • What is the plumbing concern, in plain words?
  • Where in the property is it occurring?
  • When did you first notice it, and has anything changed?
  • Is the request for maintenance, repair, or installation?
  • Is there an access detail we should know about?

Avoid taking fixtures apart or trying a repair if you don't know what the work involves. Don't leave an active problem unmentioned when booking a separate, routine task. Bring up each concern so the request reflects what you actually need. If you are arranging an installation, describe the intended use and the change you're hoping to make.
