What to have ready before a visit

A little preparation can make it easier to talk through the situation. Before calling or arranging service, consider noting:

  • The room or area where the issue is happening.
  • What you observed and when it began.
  • Whether the condition has changed since you first noticed it.
  • Any plumbing work or installation you're looking to have completed.
  • How to access the affected area, including any relevant entry details.

For a repair, describe the symptoms rather than guessing at a fix. For an installation, explain what you want changed and what the space is used for. Avoid attempting a repair you're unsure how to complete, since that may complicate the work or create another problem.
